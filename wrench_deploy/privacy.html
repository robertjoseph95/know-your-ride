Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains what information Know Your Ride (the “Service”) collects, how we use it, and your choices.

What We Collect

  • Email address — if you create an account or subscribe to Pro. Used for sign-in, subscription management, and operational email.
  • Password — stored only as a salted scrypt hash. We never see or store your plaintext password.
  • Saved vehicles, mileage, and maintenance log entries — if you use My Garage. Stored against your account so we can show them back to you.
  • Usage analytics via Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — aggregated, pseudonymous data such as pages viewed, browser, approximate location (country/region), and basic device info. We do not link analytics to your account email.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. Card details never touch our servers; we only receive a customer/subscription identifier.
  • VINs you enter — sent to our VIN decode endpoint to look up vehicle specs. VINs you do not explicitly save are not retained beyond what is necessary to serve the decode response.

How We Use It

  • To run your account and Pro subscription (sign-in, billing, restoring access on a new device).
  • To save and display your garage, mileage, and service log.
  • To understand which features are used, so we can improve the Service. Analytics are aggregated; we do not profile individuals.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or security incidents.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell personal data. We do not share your email, password hash, garage, or service log with advertisers or data brokers. We do not use your personal data to train third-party AI models.

Service Providers

We use a small set of providers to operate the Service:

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management.
  • Upstash Redis — encrypted at-rest storage for accounts, sessions, garage data, and service logs.
  • Vercel — hosting and serverless functions.
  • Google Analytics 4 — aggregated, pseudonymous usage analytics.
  • Anthropic Claude API — powers repair guides and the Know Your Part image scanner. We do not send your email or account identifiers to Anthropic.
  • NHTSA, EPA, and OEM data sources — queried server-side; no personal data is sent.

Each provider has its own privacy practices governing how it handles the limited data we share for that specific purpose.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and browser localStorage for the following purposes:

  • Strictly necessary — session token (so you stay signed in), Pro entitlement flag, dismissed-banner flag.
  • Analytics — GA4 sets first-party cookies to measure aggregate usage. You can block these via your browser settings or extensions without breaking the Service.

Data Retention

Account data and saved vehicles are retained while your account is active. Session tokens auto-expire after 30 days. You may delete your account at any time by emailing hello@knowyourride.net; we will remove your account record, garage, and logs from our active stores within 30 days of the request.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email us at hello@knowyourride.net and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and Pro subscriptions require users to be 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Security

We use industry-standard practices: HTTPS everywhere, scrypt-hashed passwords, short-lived session tokens, server-side validation of all writes, and least-privilege access to our data stores. No service can guarantee perfect security; please use a strong, unique password and notify us if you suspect compromise.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Service or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email hello@knowyourride.net.
