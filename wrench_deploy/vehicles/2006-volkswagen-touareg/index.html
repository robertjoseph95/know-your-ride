2006 Volkswagen Touareg - Electric Vehicle Guide

The 2006 Volkswagen Touareg belongs to a Volkswagen Touareg lineup that Know Your Ride tracks across 6 model years, from 2005 to 2012. It is an all-electric vehicle, so it skips the oil changes, spark plugs and exhaust-system service of a combustion Touareg entirely. It is configured as 4-wheel or all-wheel drive with an automatic (s6). The 2006 sits mid-run, with both earlier (2005) and later (2012) Touareg model years also documented.

The high-voltage battery is covered for 8 years or 100,000 miles, separate from the bumper-to-bumper warranty. Routine upkeep centers on tire rotation, DOT 4 brake fluid, G13/G12++ battery/cabin coolant, cabin air filters and brake service rather than engine oil. Know Your Ride lists 13 scheduled maintenance items for this vehicle, the earliest interval falling at 7,500 miles. Documented torque values include lug nut 89 ft-lb.
