2022 Lucid Air - Electric Vehicle Guide

It is an all-electric vehicle, so it skips the oil changes, spark plugs and exhaust-system service of a combustion Air entirely. It is configured as all-wheel drive with an automatic (a1). This 2022 car is the earliest model year on record for the Lucid Air.
