2026 Nissan Altima - Electric Vehicle Guide

The 2026 Nissan Altima belongs to a Nissan Altima lineup that Know Your Ride tracks across 29 model years, from 1993 to 2026. It is an all-electric vehicle, so it skips the oil changes, spark plugs and exhaust-system service of a combustion Altima entirely. It is configured as all-wheel drive with an automatic (variable gear ratios). This 2026 car is the most recent Nissan Altima model year on record.

The high-voltage battery is covered for 8 years or 100,000 miles, separate from the bumper-to-bumper warranty. Routine upkeep centers on tire rotation, DOT 3 brake fluid, Long Life battery/cabin coolant, cabin air filters and brake service rather than engine oil. Know Your Ride lists 20 scheduled maintenance items for this vehicle, the earliest interval falling at 5,000 miles. Documented torque values include lug nut 80 ft-lb.

Its overall reliability signal is rated "Excellent" based on aggregated complaint and crash data.

Estimated annual charging cost runs near $1,550.
