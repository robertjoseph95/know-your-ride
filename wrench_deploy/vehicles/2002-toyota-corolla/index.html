2002 Toyota Corolla - Maintenance & Specs

The 2002 Toyota Corolla belongs to a Toyota Corolla lineup that Know Your Ride tracks across 40 model years, from 1981 to 2026. Engine options on record include the 1.8L, 1.8L I4. It is configured as front-wheel drive with an automatic 3-spd. The 2002 sits mid-run, with both earlier (1981) and later (2026) Corolla model years also documented.

It takes 5W-30 full synthetic oil, 4.4 qt with a new filter meeting ILSAC GF-6A. Fluid specs on file include Super Long Life Coolant (SLLC) coolant, DOT 3 brake fluid.
