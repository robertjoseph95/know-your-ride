2010 Chrysler 300 - Maintenance & Specs

The 2010 Chrysler 300 belongs to a Chrysler 300 lineup that Know Your Ride tracks across 10 model years, from 2005 to 2026. Engine options on record include the 5.7L V8, 5.7L, 5.7L Hemi. It is configured as all-wheel drive with an automatic 5-spd. The 2010 sits mid-run, with both earlier (2005) and later (2026) 300 model years also documented.

It takes 5W-20 full synthetic oil, 7.0 qt with a new filter. Fluid specs on file include HOAT coolant, DOT 3 brake fluid, ATF+4 transmission fluid.
