2000 Isuzu Trooper - Maintenance & Specs

The 2000 Isuzu Trooper belongs to a Isuzu Trooper lineup that Know Your Ride tracks across 19 model years, from 1984 to 2002. Engine options on record include the 3.5L, 3.5L V6. It is configured as 4-wheel or all-wheel drive with an automatic 4-spd. The 2000 sits mid-run, with both earlier (1984) and later (2002) Trooper model years also documented.

It takes 5W-30 full synthetic oil, 4.5 qt with a new filter meeting API SP. Fluid specs on file include OAT Coolant coolant, DOT 3 brake fluid. Know Your Ride lists 15 scheduled maintenance items for this vehicle, the earliest interval falling at 7,500 miles. Documented torque values include oil drain plug 20 ft-lb, lug nut 80 ft-lb, spark plug 18 ft-lb.

There are 2 safety recalls on record; one covers: VEHICLE DESCRIPTION: SPORT UTILITY VEHICLES EQUIPPED WITH 4-WHEEL DRIVE AND AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSIONS BUILT WITH TOD (TORQUE-ON-DEMAND). IN CERTAIN.... Its overall reliability signal is rated "Excellent" based on aggregated complaint and crash data.

EPA fuel economy is rated at 13 city / 17 highway MPG, 15 MPG combined. Estimated annual fuel cost is about $2,900.
