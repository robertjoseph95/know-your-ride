2002 Dodge Caravan - Maintenance & Specs

The 2002 Dodge Caravan belongs to a Dodge Caravan lineup that Know Your Ride tracks across 20 model years, from 1984 to 2004. Engine options on record include the 2.4L, 3.8L, 3.3L. It is configured as front-wheel drive with an automatic 3-spd. The 2002 sits mid-run, with both earlier (1984) and later (2004) Caravan model years also documented.

It takes 5W-20 full synthetic oil, 4.5 qt with a new filter meeting MS-6395. Fluid specs on file include MOPAR OAT coolant, DOT 3 brake fluid. Know Your Ride lists 15 scheduled maintenance items for this vehicle, the earliest interval falling at 7,500 miles. Documented torque values include oil drain plug 20 ft-lb, lug nut 100 ft-lb, spark plug 18 ft-lb.

There are 10 safety recalls on record; one covers: ...USED ON 1996-2000 DAIMLERCHRYSLER TOWN & COUNTRY, DODGE CARAVAN, AND PLYMOUTH VOYAGER VEHICLES, CAN CAUSE THE FUEL LINES TO LEAK.. Its overall reliability signal is rated "Excellent" based on aggregated complaint and crash data.

EPA fuel economy is rated at 17 city / 23 highway MPG, 19 MPG combined. Estimated annual fuel cost is about $2,300.
