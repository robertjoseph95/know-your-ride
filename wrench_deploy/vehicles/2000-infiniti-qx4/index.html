2000 Infiniti QX4 - Maintenance & Specs

The 2000 Infiniti QX4 belongs to a Infiniti QX4 lineup that Know Your Ride tracks across 3 model years, from 2000 to 2002. Engine options on record include the 3.3L, 3.3L V6. It is configured as 4-wheel or all-wheel drive with an automatic 4-spd. This 2000 car is the earliest model year on record for the Infiniti QX4.

It takes 5W-30 full synthetic oil, 4.5 qt with a new filter meeting ILSAC GF-5. Fluid specs on file include OAT Coolant coolant, DOT 3 brake fluid. Know Your Ride lists 14 scheduled maintenance items for this vehicle, the earliest interval falling at 7,500 miles.

There are 3 safety recalls on record; one covers: ...EAR 1996-2004 PATHFINDER AND MODEL YEAR 1997-2003 INFINITI QX4 VEHICLES ORIGINALLY SOLD, OR CURRENTLY REGISTERED IN, CONNECTICUT, DELAWARE, IOWA....

EPA fuel economy is rated at 13 city / 17 highway MPG, 15 MPG combined. Estimated annual fuel cost is about $2,900.
