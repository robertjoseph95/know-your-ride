2000 Mitsubishi Galant - Maintenance & Specs

The 2000 Mitsubishi Galant belongs to a Mitsubishi Galant lineup that Know Your Ride tracks across 18 model years, from 1985 to 2002. Engine options on record include the 2.4L, 3.0L, 2.4L I4. It is configured as front-wheel drive with an automatic 4-spd. The 2000 sits mid-run, with both earlier (1985) and later (2002) Galant model years also documented.
